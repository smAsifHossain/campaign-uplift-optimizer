Technical methodology Open

Cleaning and validation. The official gzip archive was checksum-pinned, schema-validated and scanned in chunks. All expected columns were present, binary outcome fields were verified, and no missing values were found in the analysis sample.

Leakage control. Twelve anonymized features were hashed together. Identical feature vectors stayed in the same 60/20/20 partition.

Modeling. A Linear T-Learner fits separate logistic regressions to treatment and control. Its difference in predicted conversion probability is the customer-level uplift score. Boosted T- and S-Learners were evaluated as causal alternatives; conversion propensity and random targeting served as policy baselines.

Selection and evaluation. The causal model and campaign reach were selected using validation AUUC above random. Final treatment effects, policy curves and bootstrap intervals were reported once on the locked test set.
