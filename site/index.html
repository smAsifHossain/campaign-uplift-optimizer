About the data

Criteo Uplift Prediction Dataset v2.1

Each row is one anonymized observation from a randomized advertising incrementality test. Treatment records were assigned to advertising; control records were held back. The dataset contains no names, contact details, ad creative, product identity, or revenue.

Conversion is the primary outcome and visit is a secondary outcome. Raw records are not redistributed in this repository.

Data and quality

Verify the source before analysis

What was done
The official compressed archive was pinned to a source commit, checked against its expected byte size and SHA-256 checksum, opened to verify gzip integrity, and required to contain all sixteen expected columns. Treatment, conversion, and visit were validated as binary fields.
Why it matters
These checks prevent a partial, altered, or structurally incompatible file from silently entering the analysis.

Data preparation

Separate learning, selection, and final reporting

The twelve anonymized feature values are hashed together. Every identical feature vector receives the same hash bucket, so matching covariate groups stay in one split and cannot leak across boundaries.

Randomized experiment

Establish whether advertising worked on average

What was done
The observed conversion rate for randomized treatment records was compared with the rate for control records. No predictive model is needed for this comparison because treatment assignment is supplied by the experiment.
Why it matters
The average treatment effect is the causal benchmark that every targeting policy must remain consistent with at full reach.

Modeling and selection

Rank customers by incremental response

What was done
Three auditable causal candidates were trained: a regularized logistic T-Learner, a boosted T-Learner, and a boosted S-Learner. A conversion-propensity model and random targeting provide non-causal comparison policies.
How selection worked
Each candidate ranked the validation set. AUUC above random measures the incremental conversions accumulated as more of that ranked audience is targeted. Higher validation AUUC indicates a more useful causal ranking.
Result
— was selected among the causal candidates. Validation data also selected a — campaign reach.

Locked-test evaluation

Measure policy value on unseen data

What was reported
The selected model scored the locked test set once. Randomized treatment assignments support policy curves, 95% uncertainty intervals, calibration by uplift rank, and paired comparisons against propensity and random targeting.
Why it matters
This evaluates the targeting decision itself: whether customers ranked near the top actually show more incremental response on unseen experimental data.

Business decision

Convert lift into a transparent scenario

What was done
The measured policy curve is combined with eligible population, contact cost, conversion value, and maximum reach. Criteo does not supply monetary values, so every dollar figure is explicitly a user-controlled scenario.
Calculation
Scenario profit equals estimated incremental conversions multiplied by assumed value per conversion, minus targeted customers multiplied by assumed contact cost.
Default scenario
At the validation-selected reach, the locked-test estimate is — after campaign contact cost. The recommendation remains a controlled rollout with a permanent randomized holdout.
